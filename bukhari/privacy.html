Sahih Al Bukhari – Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: Sahih Al Bukhari has no internet permission, no account and no server. Everything you do in it stays on your phone, and Digital Beaver doesn't collect, see or sell anything.

What the app stores

All the hadiths come inside the app. On your phone it keeps only:

  • the hadiths you bookmark and the ones you have opened (for "Umesoma" and "Endelea");
  • your settings, such as the Arabic font, text sizes, theme and reminder time.

Other apps can't read this. If Android's own backup is on for your phone, these settings may be included in it; the hadith texts are not.

What we collect

Nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. The app can't send anything anywhere because it doesn't ask for the internet permission. Links such as the Deen page open in your browser only when you tap them.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on the daily "hadithi ya leo" reminder.
  • Run at start-up: to set the reminder again after the phone restarts.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything.

Content

The Kiswahili translations, explanations and lessons are from HadeethEnc.com (Rowwad Translation Center), unchanged. The Arabic of Sahih al-Bukhari is checked word for word against sunnah.com.

Children

Sahih Al Bukhari is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
