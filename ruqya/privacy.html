Ruqya Shariyya – Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: Ruqya Shariyya has no account and no server. It uses the internet only to download the recitation audio once, and Digital Beaver doesn't collect, see or sell anything.

What the app stores

The verses and supplications come inside the app. On your phone it keeps:

  • the recitation audio, after you choose to download it;
  • your progress (which routines you finished and when) and the adhkar you chose;
  • your settings, such as language, translation, font, text size and reminder times.

Other apps can't read this. If Android's own backup is on for your phone, your settings and progress may be included in it; the audio is not.

The internet

The only download is the recitation audio (Mishary Alafasy) from everyayah.com, when you ask for it. Like any download, that server sees your phone's internet address; the app sends nothing else.

What we collect

Nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.

Permissions

  • Internet: to download the recitation audio once.
  • Notifications (optional): for the morning, evening and bedtime reminders you turn on, and the playback controls.
  • Run at start-up: to set reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Media playback service: so recitation keeps playing with the screen off.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything, including the audio.

Children

Ruqya Shariyya is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
