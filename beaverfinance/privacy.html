Beaver Finance – Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

In short: your money records stay on your phone, encrypted. Backups you choose to send to Google Drive are encrypted before they leave the phone. Digital Beaver has no server, no account system and doesn't collect, see or sell anything. Text messages are read only if you turn that on, and only on the phone; message text goes to an AI service only if you add your own key for one.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays in an encrypted database in the app's private storage on your phone: accounts, transactions, clients, invoices, quotations, projects, contracts, budgets, notes and your settings. Logos, receipt photos and documents you add are kept in the same private storage. Other apps can't read it, and Android's own cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Backups and Google Drive

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, you sign in with your Google account and the app saves an encrypted backup file in a hidden app folder of your Drive. It can only see the files it made there, not the rest of your Drive. The file is encrypted on the phone (AES-256) with a key locked by your backup passphrase and your recovery code, so neither Google nor Digital Beaver can open it.

If you turn on Use on two phones, both phones read and write those same encrypted files in your Drive to take turns. Nothing passes through any other server.

You can also save or share a backup file yourself; it goes only where you send it.

Wallet messages (SMS)

If you turn on Wallet messages, the app reads confirmation messages from mobile money and bank senders (M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, Selcom Pesa and CRDB) to turn them into transactions for you to check. Messages from any other sender are skipped on the phone and never read by the app. What it reads is kept in the same encrypted database as the rest of your records and is never sent to Digital Beaver.

With message alerts on, the app also notices when a message that looks like a money confirmation arrives from an ordinary phone number, and warns you that it may be fake. Only that warning is kept, on the phone.

You can instead share or paste a message into the app yourself. You can turn reading off at any time in the app, or take the permission away in Android's settings.

AI sorting (optional)

AI sorting is off unless you add your own API key from an AI service you choose: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini), or another service you enter by its web address. When it is on, wallet messages the app can't sort by itself are sent to that service to suggest what they are, together with the names of your expense categories, income categories, clients and suppliers so it can choose among them. Phone numbers, account numbers and card numbers are removed before sending. This goes directly from your phone to the service you chose, under your own key and account, and that service's own privacy terms apply to it. To show you which models you can pick, the app also asks that service for its list of models. The key is kept in the phone's protected storage and is never put in backups. Turn AI sorting off in the app to remove the key.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and no account with Digital Beaver.

Sharing

Invoices, quotations, receipts and reports are made as PDFs on the phone. They go only where you share them, for example WhatsApp or email. Tapping a client's phone or email opens your own phone, WhatsApp or email app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for Google Drive backups, the two-phone option, and AI sorting if you turn it on.
  • Read and receive SMS: only if you turn on wallet messages, to read confirmations from the mobile money and bank senders listed above and to warn you about fake ones. The app never sends SMS.
  • Fingerprint or face, and your screen lock: to lock the app with your phone's own lock, if you leave that on. The app never sees your fingerprint or PIN; Android checks them.
  • Notifications, run at startup and keep awake: for reminders such as due invoices and to-dos, which are made on the phone.
  • Photos and files: the app opens only the logo, receipt or document you pick. It never browses your files or photos on its own.

Deleting your data

Delete records in the app, or uninstall Beaver Finance to remove everything on the phone. To delete backups in Google Drive, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, Manage apps, find Beaver Finance and choose Delete hidden app data.

Children

Beaver Finance is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes
