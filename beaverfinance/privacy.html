Beaver Finance – Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

In short: your money records stay on your phone, encrypted. Backups you choose to send to Google Drive are encrypted before they leave the phone. Digital Beaver has no server, no account system and doesn't collect, see or sell anything.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays in an encrypted database in the app's private storage on your phone: accounts, transactions, clients, invoices, quotations, projects, contracts, budgets, notes and your settings. Logos, receipt photos and documents you add are kept in the same private storage. Other apps can't read it, and Android's own cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Backups and Google Drive

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, you sign in with your Google account and the app saves an encrypted backup file in a hidden app folder of your Drive. It can only see the files it made there, not the rest of your Drive. The file is encrypted on the phone (AES-256) with a key locked by your backup passphrase and your recovery code, so neither Google nor Digital Beaver can open it.

If you turn on Use on two phones, both phones read and write those same encrypted files in your Drive to take turns. Nothing passes through any other server.

You can also save or share a backup file yourself; it goes only where you send it.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and no account with Digital Beaver.

Sharing

Invoices, quotations, receipts and reports are made as PDFs on the phone. They go only where you share them, for example WhatsApp or email. Tapping a client's phone or email opens your own phone, WhatsApp or email app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for Google Drive backups and the two-phone option.
  • Fingerprint or face, and your screen lock: to lock the app with your phone's own lock, if you leave that on. The app never sees your fingerprint or PIN; Android checks them.
  • Notifications, run at startup and keep awake: for reminders such as due invoices and to-dos, which are made on the phone.
  • Photos and files: the app opens only the logo, receipt or document you pick. It never browses your files or photos on its own.

Deleting your data

Delete records in the app, or uninstall Beaver Finance to remove everything on the phone. To delete backups in Google Drive, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, Manage apps, find Beaver Finance and choose Delete hidden app data.

Children

Beaver Finance is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
