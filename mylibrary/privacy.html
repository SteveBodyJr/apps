My Library – Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

In short: your books stay on your phone. My Library has no internet permission, no account and no server, and Digital Beaver doesn't collect, see or sell anything.

What the app stores

When you add a book, My Library keeps its own copy of the PDF in the app's private storage on your phone, so the book still opens if the original file is moved or deleted. It also keeps:

  • each book's title, author, cover and the shelf it is on;
  • your reading progress (the page you're on and when you last opened it);
  • your recent searches and your settings, such as paper colour, shelf style and page sounds.

Other apps can't read this storage, and Android's cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Adding books and covers

My Library opens only the files you pick, share to it or open with it. If you choose a photo as a book's cover, Android's photo picker lets you pick one picture, and My Library sees only that picture. It never browses your files or photos on its own.

What we collect

Nothing. My Library has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. It doesn't ask for the internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.

Permissions

None. The app needs no special permissions.

Deleting your data

Remove a book in the app to delete its copy, cover and progress. Uninstall My Library to remove everything on the phone. Your original files are never changed or deleted by the app.

Children

My Library is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
