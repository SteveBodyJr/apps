Folio – Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

In short: your documents stay on your phone, encrypted. If you turn on Google Drive backup, an encrypted copy also goes to a private folder in your own Google Drive. Digital Beaver doesn't collect, see or sell anything.

What the app stores

Folio keeps the documents you add (photos, scans, PDFs, Word and other files, cards, signatures) in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it.

  • Encrypted: every file, scan, card picture and signature, plus each document's number and notes. They are locked with a key that only your passcode (or your fingerprint, through Android) can open.
  • Not encrypted, but private to Folio: each document's type, name, owner, issuer and dates. Folio needs these while it is locked, to send expiry reminders and to fill the home-screen widget.

Passcode and fingerprint

Folio never stores your passcode. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; Folio never sees your fingerprint or face. Folio blocks screenshots by default and locks itself after you leave it. The recovery code is shown to you once, and you keep it.

Home-screen widget (optional)

The "Expiring next" widget shows document types and expiry dates, for example "Passport, 12 days left". It shows document names only if you turn on "Names on the widget". It never shows files, numbers or notes.

Backups

  • Backups are encrypted with a backup password you choose. Without that password, a backup file can't be opened, by anyone, including Digital Beaver.
  • You can save a backup file anywhere you pick on your phone.
  • Only when you tap Back up to Google Drive does Folio ask Google for the drive.appdata permission: a hidden folder in your Drive that only Folio can use. Folio can't see your other Drive files. If you turn on automatic backup, Folio uploads a new encrypted backup after you change something (on Wi-Fi only, unless you allow mobile data).
  • Backups go straight from your phone to your Google account. They don't pass through any Digital Beaver server.
  • You can delete them in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Folio → Delete hidden app data) and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Opening files from other apps

When you open a file with Folio from another app (for example WhatsApp or Files), Folio shows it without saving it. It is added to your encrypted documents only if you tap Save to Folio. Converting a file to PDF, and filling in or signing one, happens on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Folio has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Digital Beaver has no server that receives your data.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and cards. Pictures go straight into Folio's encrypted storage, not your gallery.
  • Notifications: expiry reminders and backup problems.
  • Alarms & reminders: so reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup: to set reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: only for Google Drive backup, and to check for Wi-Fi before an automatic backup.
  • Fingerprint / face: only to unlock Folio when you choose it.

Sharing

Folio shares a document only when you tap Share, and only with the app you pick. The link it gives that app stops working when Folio locks.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app, or uninstall Folio to remove everything on the phone. Backup files you saved yourself, and Google Drive backups, are deleted where they are stored (see above).

Children

Folio is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
