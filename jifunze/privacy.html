Jifunze Quran – Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 (version 1.2.0)

In short: Jifunze Quran has no account, no ads and no analytics. It uses the internet only to download Sheikh al-Husary's recitation of Juz 'Amma, and the microphone only if a teacher records lessons. Nothing you do in it is sent to Digital Beaver or anyone else.

What the app stores

The lessons, the Juz 'Amma text and the recitation used in the lessons come inside the app. On your phone it keeps only:

  • your progress: lessons you have finished, the last lesson you opened, your best quiz score and your reminder time;
  • Juz 'Amma ayat you have listened to or saved, so they play again without internet;
  • in teacher mode, the sounds you record, and sound packs you import from a teacher.

Other apps can't read this. If Android's own backup is on for your phone, your progress and recordings may be included in it.

Internet

The first time you play an ayah of Juz 'Amma, the app downloads Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary's recitation of that ayah from everyayah.com and keeps it on the phone. That request contains only which ayah is needed. Nothing else is sent, and the rest of the app works offline.

Microphone (teacher mode only)

Teacher mode is off unless you turn it on. Only then does the app ask to use the microphone, to record the sound of each letter and word. Recordings stay on your phone. They leave it only when you tap "Tuma sauti" and choose where to send the sound pack yourself, for example to your students on WhatsApp.

Notifications (optional)

Only if you turn on the daily "Somo la leo" reminder: the app asks to show notifications and sets an alarm on your phone for the time you choose. The reminder is made on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere. The app also asks to run at start-up, only to set the reminder again after the phone restarts.

What we collect

Nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. Links such as the Deen page open in your browser only when you tap them.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything, including recordings and saved recitation.

Content

The Quran text is from Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), unchanged. The recitation is by Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary (everyayah.com); the timing of each word is from quran.com.

Children

Jifunze Quran is made for learners of every age, including children. It doesn't collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
