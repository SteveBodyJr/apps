You need an account · Unahitaji akaunti

DukaPOS is for shops that use DukaPOS. Sign in with the username and password your shop owner gave you.
Programu hii ni kwa maduka yanayotumia DukaPOS. Ingia kwa jina na nenosiri ulilopewa na mwenye duka.

What you can do · Unachoweza kufanya

Sell, even offlineSales are saved on the phone and sent when the internet comes back. · Uza hata bila mtandao.
Products and stockAdd products, receive stock, scan barcodes, see what is running low. · Bidhaa na stoku.
Customers and debtsWho owes what, record payments, send statements on WhatsApp. · Wateja na madeni.
ReportsSales, profit and loss, items report, stock report. · Ripoti za mauzo na faida.
Cash register and expensesOpen and close the drawer, record what the shop spends. · Droo ya fedha na matumizi.
English and KiswahiliEvery branch, every role, in your language. · Kiingereza na Kiswahili.

Installing · Kusakinisha

  1. Tap Download APK on this phone. Gusa Pakua APK kwenye simu hii.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Fungua faili iliyopakuliwa.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then go back and tap Install. Android ikiuliza, ruhusu kivinjari kusakinisha programu, kisha ugusa Install.
  4. Updating? Install over the old version. Your account and unsent sales stay. Unasasisha? Sakinisha juu ya toleo la zamani; mauzo yako yanabaki.

DukaPOS isn't on Google Play yet, so Play Protect may ask you to confirm. · Bado haiko Google Play, kwa hiyo Play Protect inaweza kukuuliza uthibitishe.
