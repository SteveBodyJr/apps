Until – Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: your moments stay on your phone. If you turn on Google Drive backup, they are also copied to a private folder in your own Google Drive. Digital Beaver doesn't collect, see or sell anything.

What the app stores

Until keeps the moments you add (titles, dates, notes, categories, prices, reminders, colours and any photo you choose for a card) in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

  • Only when you tap Connect Google Drive, Until asks Google for the drive.appdata permission: a hidden folder in your Drive that only Until can use. Until can't see your other Drive files.
  • Backups go straight from your phone to your Google account. They don't pass through any Digital Beaver server.
  • You can delete them in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Until → Delete hidden app data) and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What we collect

Nothing. Until has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Digital Beaver has no server that receives your data.

To tell you about new versions, Until reads the public list of releases at stevebodyjr.github.io/apps (hosted by GitHub). That request sends nothing about you or your moments; like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders, renewals, milestones and new versions.
  • Alarms & reminders (if you allow it): so reminders arrive on the exact minute.
  • Run at startup: to set reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: only for Google Drive backup and the update check.
  • Photos: Until never asks for access to your gallery. When you pick a photo with Android's photo picker, only that photo is copied into the app.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a moment, Until makes a picture of it and hands it to the app you choose. Nothing is shared otherwise.

Deleting your data

Delete a moment in the app, or uninstall Until to remove everything on the phone. Backup files you saved yourself, and Google Drive backups, are deleted where they are stored (see above).

Children

Until is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
